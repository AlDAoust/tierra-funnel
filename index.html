One list, tracked by tags

One Kajabi list. Each stage has its own tag (see the table above); emails follow the tags.

Never registered: has bf-invite-oct29 but not bf-webinar-registrant. Weekly email CTA: save my seat for the next webinar.

Registered, applied or waitlist: has bf-webinar-registrant, founding-applicant or cohort-waitlist. Weekly email: primary CTA apply; secondary CTA next webinar (mainly for no-shows; attendees only when the topic is new). Waitlist gets first access when the next cohort opens.

Weekly boost loop

Starts after the Nov 5 webinar. Webinar → clips → LinkedIn content → best performer? Yes: boost (paid), which drives to webinar registration. No: keep posting organically.

Rule: once a week, boost the single best-performing clip from that week.
