The funnel

One list, two segments

One Kajabi list, two segments by tag. One weekly teaching email from Alex goes to both; the CTA differs by tag.

Segment 1, never registered (most of the current list of about 1,158): CTA save my seat for the next webinar.

Segment 2, been through a webinar (attended or registered but didn't buy, plus waitlist): CTA apply first, save my seat as backup. Waitlist gets first access when the next cohort opens.

Everyone starts in segment 1 and moves to segment 2 once they've been through a webinar.

Weekly boost loop

Starts after the Nov 5 webinar. Webinar → clips → LinkedIn content → best performer? Yes: boost (paid), which drives to webinar registration. No: keep posting organically.

Rule: once a week, boost the single best-performing clip from that week.

Criteria: TBD with Alex. Proposed starting signals: clicks to the registration page (these matter most), and engagement rate compared with Alex's usual posts.
